# Code of conduct

This project welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Participation in repository issues, pull requests, and other project spaces requires respectful behavior.

## Expected behavior

- Critique ideas and code constructively, not the person presenting them.
- Be patient with questions, explain relevant context, and accept feedback.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's contact details or private information without permission.
- Keep discussion relevant to the project and acknowledge others' work.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and sharing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately to **arminayat3@gmail.com**. Include links and enough context to assess the issue; do not publish a report that exposes someone else's private information. Reports are handled as confidentially as practical.

The maintainer may request changes, remove content, limit participation, or ban participants according to the seriousness and repetition of the behavior. Good-faith disagreement about technical decisions is not misconduct. These expectations apply equally to maintainers and contributors.
